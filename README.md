### 🧠 [AI-Assisted Early Detection of Alzheimer's Disease from Brain MRI](https://github.com/Abtahi360/AI-Assisted-Early-Detection-of-Alzheimer-s-Disease-from-Brain-MRI)

A deep learning system that classifies brain MRI scans into **Alzheimer's disease stages** (from no impairment to moderate decline). It is designed to *support* clinical decisions, not replace them.

**Highlights:** dual-perspective deep learning · model explainability · privacy-aware design for siloed hospital data

![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Manuscript_in_Preparation-yellow?style=flat-square)

### 💔 [Explainable ML Framework for Post-Breakup Psychological Distress](https://github.com/Abtahi360/post-breakup-distress-ml-framework)

A hybrid, **leakage-audited and explainable** ML framework that predicts post-breakup distress, recovery, and suicide risk from a survey of **4,333 participants**.

**Highlights:** explainable AI · strict leakage auditing · transparent and honest reporting
